Privacy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This policy explains how my apps and this site handle your information. It applies to all of my apps and this website.

What I collect

My apps are built to collect as little as possible. Depending on the app, that may include:

  • Information you provide — such as text, photos or notes you enter to use a feature. In Home Survival Kit, this stays on your device, or in your own cloud storage if you choose to back it up there.
  • Basic diagnostics — anonymous crash and performance data that Apple or Google may share with developers if you allow it in your device settings.
  • Contact details — only if you email support, and only to reply.

What I don't do

  • I don't sell your personal information.
  • I don't run third-party ads that track you across other apps.
  • I don't collect anything beyond what's described here.

Third-party services

My apps are distributed through Apple's App Store and Google Play, and Photostrator 3D runs on the web. Features that need an outside service, such as AI features, send only the information needed to provide that feature. These providers process data only to deliver their service.

Children

My apps aren't directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect their personal information.

Your choices

You can request a copy or deletion of any personal data I hold by emailing the address below, and you can turn off analytics in your device settings where supported.
