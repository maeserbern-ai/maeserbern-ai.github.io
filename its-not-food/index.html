Fewer clues, more points.

From the Ingredient Dictionary series by Maeser

How to play

  1. Split into teams. Teams take turns.
  2. You start with the first 3 ingredients — they're listed from most to least.
  3. Stuck? Reveal another ingredient. Each reveal is worth one point less.
  4. Pick an answer. Right = points. Wrong = zero.
  5. Watch out — it might not be food at all.

Use the arrows on your remote and press OK. On a keyboard, 1–4 picks an answer and R reveals.
