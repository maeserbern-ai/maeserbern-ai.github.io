How to play

  • Split into teams and pick your colors. The TV shuffles who goes first. No arguing.
  • Read the 3 ingredients and guess the product. Snack or Shampoo?
  • Look it up — free. Select any ingredient to see its definition. It costs nothing.
  • Need another clue? Press Next ingredient or select a covered box to reveal one more (−1 point).
  • Right answer = points. Wrong answer = zero. And a little shame.
  • Steal! Miss it and the first team to yell “STEAL!” tries for half the points. Miss, lose them.
  • Red ingredients are worth knowing about. Amber ones are kept secret.
  • Points go up every round, and your last round counts double. Comebacks happen.
  • Hard mode starts with the weird chemicals and pays more. Sweating optional.
  • Timer (optional): Easy 60 sec, Medium 45, Hard 30, steals 10. Buy +15 sec once.
  • Finale: bet your points on toilet paper trivia. Lowest score does a dare. Yes, really.

Remote: arrows to move, the center OK button to pick. Pass it to the team whose turn it is. Back pauses the game.
