The Party Game

Fewer clues, more points.

From the Ingredient Dictionary book series by Maeser

How to play

  1. Pick an Ingredient Dictionary book — each one is its own deck — or mix them all. What Is Toilet Paper? plays as true or false.
  2. Split into teams. Teams take turns.
  3. Pick a difficulty. Easy starts at the top of the label, Medium in the middle, Hard with the tiny chemicals at the bottom. Medium questions are worth +1 point, Hard +2.
  4. Every game ends with a toilet paper true-or-false finale — one question per team, for double points.
  5. You start with the first 3 ingredients — they're listed from most to least.
  6. Stuck? Reveal another ingredient. Each reveal is worth one point less.
  7. Pick an answer. Right = points. Wrong = zero.
  8. Points go up +1 every round, and each team's final round is worth double.
  9. Don't know an ingredient? Tap it for help to see what it is. Each one you look up costs 1 point.
  10. Red ingredients are worth knowing about. Amber ones hide what's really inside.
  11. Watch out — it might not be food at all.

Use the arrows on your remote and press OK. On a keyboard, 1–4 picks an answer and R reveals.
