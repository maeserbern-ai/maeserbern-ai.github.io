How to play

Pick your poison

Not literally. Probably. Choose an Ingredient Dictionary book, or mix them all for maximum chaos. Split into teams.

Read the label

You get 3 ingredients. Want more clues? Reveal one, or tap one to learn what on earth it is. Each costs a point.

Snack or Shampoo?

Guess the product. Right = points. Wrong = shame. Red means "hmm." Amber means "they won't tell you."

Pick your pain

Easy shows the obvious stuff. Hard starts with chemicals you can't pronounce — and pays more. Add a timer if you like sweating.

Stakes go up

Every round is worth more than the last, and your final round counts double. Comebacks are totally legal.

Toilet paper finale

Bet your points on one true-or-false question about toilet paper. Yes, really. Loser does a dare.

Remote: arrows + OK. Menu or Back pauses, mutes or switches games. Keyboard: 1–4 answer, R reveal, M mute.
