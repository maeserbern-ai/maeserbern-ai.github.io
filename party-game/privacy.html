Privacy Policy

Ingredient Dictionary The Party Game, by Maeser. Last updated October 20, 2026.

The short version

The game does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics.

What stays on your device

The game saves a few settings and game details on your own device (for example sound and music on/off, and which puzzles you have already played) so the game remembers them next time. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. You can clear it at any time by clearing the app's or browser's data.

Links

The game may show links to the Ingredient Dictionary books. If you choose to open one, you will leave the game, and the site you visit has its own privacy policy.

Children

The game is suitable for families. Because we do not collect any personal information, we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
